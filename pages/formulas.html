ალგებრა

\(a + b = b + a\)
\(a - b = a + (-b)\)
\(a \cdot b = b \cdot a = ab\)
\(\frac{a}{b} = a \cdot \frac{1}{b}, \quad b \neq 0\)
\(a(b + c) = ab + ac\)
\(1 + 2 + \dots + n = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}, \quad n \in \mathbb{N}\)
\(1^2 + 2^2 + \dots + n^2 = \frac{n(n+1)(2n+1)}{6}\)
\(1^3 + 2^3 + \dots + n^3 = \left(\frac{n(n+1)}{2}\right)^2\)

შემოკლებული გამრავლების ფორმულები

\( (a + b)^2 = a^2 + 2 a b + b^2 \)
\( (a - b)^2 = a^2 - 2 a b + b^2 \)
\( a^2 - b^2 = (a - b)(a + b) \)
\( (a + b)^3 = a^3 + 3 a^2 b + 3 a b^2 + b^3 \)
\( (a - b)^3 = a^3 - 3 a^2 b + 3 a b^2 - b^3 \)
\( a^3 + b^3 = (a + b)(a^2 - a b + b^2) \)
\( a^3 - b^3 = (a - b)(a^2 + a b + b^2) \)

ხარისხები

\( a^1 = a \)
\( a^2 = a \cdot a \)
\( a^3 = a \cdot a \cdot a \)
\( a^0 = 1 \)
\( 0^0 = 1 \)
\( a^m \cdot a^n = a^{m+n} \)
\( \frac{a^m}{a^n} = a^{m-n} \)
\( (a^m)^n = a^{m \cdot n} \)
\( a^{-n} = \frac{1}{a^n} \)
\( a^{\frac{n}{m}} = \sqrt[m]{a^n} \)

ფესვები

$$\sqrt{x^2} = |x|$$
$$x^2 = a \implies x = \pm \sqrt{a}$$
$$\sqrt[3]{x^3} = x$$
$$\sqrt[m]{x^n} = x^{\frac{n}{m}}$$
$$\sqrt[n]{x^m} \cdot \sqrt[n]{x^k} = \sqrt[n]{x^{m+k}}$$
$$\sqrt[n]{x^m} / \sqrt[n]{x^k} = \sqrt[n]{x^{m-k}}$$
$$\sqrt[n]{(\sqrt[n]{x^m})^k} = \sqrt[n \cdot k]{x^{m \cdot k}}$$
$$\sqrt{(x+y)^2} = |x+y|$$
$$\sqrt{(x-y)^2} = |x-y|$$
$$\sqrt[3]{(x+y)^3} = x+y$$
$$\sqrt[3]{(x-y)^3} = x-y$$
$$\sqrt[3]{x^3 + y^3} = \sqrt[3]{(x+y)(x^2 - xy + y^2)}$$
$$\sqrt[3]{x^3 - y^3} = \sqrt[3]{(x-y)(x^2 + xy + y^2)}$$
